      Turns alternate; the very first turn has 1 action, all later turns 2 actions. An action is a MOVE (swipe the board / D-pad / arrows / WASD, PULL toggle or Shift = also pull) of one of your YOU things — tap a thing or Tab to select which — or a PLACE of a level tile into an empty cell next to one of your YOU things (tap tile, then a green cell). ALL IS PUSH — pushing shoves whole rows/columns of things along; only the board edge stops a push. PULL drags just one thing. A rule forms when a noun tile points right or down into a property tile. Defaults: ALL IS PUSH plus IS Pn for each enabled character (🐻‍❄️ Sami = P1, 🦙 Erik = P2, …). Win: a YOUR thing (P1's, P2's, …) shares a space with a WIN thing. Challenge (C): the next player gets 10 actions to win or you win. If you cannot take your actions, you lose. Undo (Z) takes back the last action. Word tiles are not objects — you win by touching the actual flag, not the word. The 👥 buttons toggle characters on/off in the same game — active players become P1, P2, … in the order listed, and new ones spawn as close to their corner as possible. The blue river is RIVER terrain (not a tile) — later rules will interact with it. Permanent tiles always sit in the margin: the P1 noun over IS P1 on the left and IS P1 below (plus the P2 noun + IS P2 on top and IS P2 on the right whenever 2+ characters are active) — you can place properties next to the printed nouns (e.g. IS FAST right of the P1 noun) to rule them. CURIOUS: when anything enters a CURIOUS thing's row or column (from outside it), it moves 1 space toward them — effects cascade. FAST: press a direction, then tap a highlighted square to slide there (swiping is ignored for FAST). If several things are YOU, tap one (or Tab) to choose which to move. Only one YOU thing sharing a space with WIN is needed to win.
