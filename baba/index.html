RULE TILES (TAP, THEN PLACE ON MAP)

BABA VS KEKE

👥 players:
Goal: move a thing that is YOU onto a thing that is WIN.
Sudden death: skip your turn — the opponent gets 10 actions to win, or you win.
Differences from the video game:
  • everything is always PUSH, but only one thing per move (unless it is STRONG)
  • things adjacent to YOU can always be PULLed (unless SCARED)
  • only one YOU thing moves per action
  • being both YOU and WIN does not win by itself
  • placing a tile does not trigger CURIOUS (SCARED does react to being placed next to it)
  • a rule loop of 100 steps = the last actor loses
  • nouns can never become other nouns
  • property tiles include the word "is"
Properties:
  • WIN — a YOU thing sharing a space with it wins.
  • CURIOUS — when anything enters its row/column, it steps 1 space toward it (effects cascade; placing a tile doesn't trigger it).
  • DEFEAT — destroys YOU things sharing its cell; no YOU things left = you lose.
  • FAST — slides as many spaces as you like in one direction.
  • HOT — destroys MELT things it touches.
  • MELT — destroyed when touching HOT (a HOT+MELT thing melts itself).
  • OPEN — can be entered (not pushed).
  • RIVER — noun for the river terrain (e.g. RIVER IS WIN).
  • SCARED — when something enters a space directly next to it (not diagonally; also when placed/pushed there), it flees as far as possible away; paralyzed if the direction is ambiguous; cannot be pulled unless the puller is STRONG.
  • SHEEP — when a SHEEP thing moves, all other SHEEP things move the same amount in the same direction if possible.
  • SHUT — like STOP, but an OPEN thing touching it opens it.
  • SINK — destroyed when sharing a cell with anything.
  • SQUARE — noun for property tiles currently in a rule (e.g. SQUARE IS PLAYER 1 lets you move an IS WIN tile out of ROCK IS WIN to repeal it).
  • STOP — can't be entered, pushed or pulled (STRONG can push/pull it).
  • STRONG — can push several things at once (even STOP/shut); a thing pushed against the board edge is pushed off and destroyed (WRAP wraps instead); can pull unpullable things.
  • TEXT — noun for ALL word tiles (e.g. TEXT IS PLAYER 1 makes them movable).
  • THIRSTY — at the end of the turn, if not on the river, it moves in one straight direction to the nearest river cell (you choose if several are equally close).
  • WRAP — pushed/moved off the grid, it wraps to the other side (unless blocked); the printed margin tiles don't block it.
Controls: tap a bordered thing to select it, then tap a walk cell to move; arrows/WASD/swipe/D-pad also work; keys 1–9, 0, q–p pick a rule tile; N = new game. Pick 0–2 rule cards below the board.
