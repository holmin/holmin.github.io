      Turns alternate; the very first turn has 1 action, all later turns 2 actions. An action is a MOVE (swipe the board / D-pad / arrows / WASD, PULL toggle or Shift = also pull) of one of your YOU things — tap a thing or Tab to select which — or a PLACE of a level tile into an empty cell next to one of your YOU things (tap tile or keys 1–9, 0, q–p, then a green cell). ALL IS PUSH — pushing shoves whole rows/columns of things along; only the board edge stops a push. PULL drags just one thing. A rule forms when a noun tile points right or down into a property tile. Defaults: ALL IS PUSH plus IS PLAYER n for each enabled character (🐻‍❄️ Sami = PLAYER 1, 🦙 Aas = PLAYER 2, …). Videogame property tiles in the supply: STOP (blocks entry and can't be pushed/pulled), SHUT (same, but an OPEN thing touching it opens it; OPEN things can be entered), WEAK (destroyed when pushed/pulled), SINK (destroyed when sharing a cell), HOT+MELT (melt things touching hot things), DEFEAT (destroys YOU things it touches), plus RIVER and TEXT nouns — RIVER rules the river terrain, and TEXT rules the word tiles themselves (e.g. TEXT IS PLAYER 1 makes all word tiles PLAYER 1's YOU things, so you can move them). Win: a YOUR thing (PLAYER 1's, PLAYER 2's, …) shares a space with a WIN thing. Walk by tapping a green dashed-outline cell (if your thing is FAST, all squares it can slide to are highlighted); placing a tile shows gold dashed-outline cells instead. The outline never changes the cell background, so the river stays visible. Tap one of your things (bordered) to select it — a cell containing your own thing always selects it, it is never a walk target. Swipe/D-pad/arrows/WASD also still work. The level dropdown picks which TWO rule tiles are in the supply (per the rulebook); ALL RULES (developer) at the bottom is preselected for now. Challenge (C): the next player gets 10 actions to win or you win. If you cannot take your actions, you lose. Undo (Z) takes back the last action. Word tiles are not objects — you win by touching the actual flag, not the word. The 👥 buttons toggle characters on/off in the same game — active players become PLAYER 1, PLAYER 2, … in the order listed, and new ones spawn as close to their rank's corner as possible (PLAYER 1 lower-left, PLAYER 2 upper-right, …). The blue river is RIVER terrain (not a tile) — later rules will interact with it. Permanent tiles always sit in the margin: the PLAYER-1 noun over IS PLAYER 1 on the left and IS PLAYER 1 below (plus the PLAYER-2 noun + IS PLAYER 2 on top and IS PLAYER 2 on the right whenever 2+ characters are active) — you can place properties next to the printed nouns (e.g. IS FAST right of the PLAYER-1 noun) to rule them. CURIOUS: when anything enters a CURIOUS thing's row or column (from outside it), it moves 1 space toward them — effects cascade. FAST: press a direction, then tap a highlighted square to slide there (swiping is ignored for FAST). If several things are YOU, tap one (or Tab) to choose which to move. Only one YOU thing sharing a space with WIN is needed to win.
