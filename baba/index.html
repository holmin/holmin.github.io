BABA VS KEKE

Rules

    Log

      Turns alternate; the very first turn has 1 action, all later turns 2 actions. An action is a MOVE (arrows/WASD, Shift = also PULL) of one of your YOU things — Tab or click to select which — or a PLACE of a level tile into an empty cell next to one of your YOU things. Everything is PUSH; pushed things need an empty cell behind them. A rule forms when a noun tile points right or down into a property tile. Win: a thing that is YOU shares a space with a thing that is WIN. If you think the game is unwinnable, challenge (C): your opponent gets 10 actions to win or you win. If you cannot take your actions, you lose.
